what is bullpov?

bullpov is a platform where people will predict skillfully on whether
a share will rise of fall on the same day.

we will display more than 50 shares from indian stock market - Bull50

vision - making stock market more fun to explore and invest. we are trying 
to build more excitement amongst netizens about stock market. we also aim to make 
people keep a keen eye on economic and finacial news so that they can have the best
knowledge about the indian economy and financial markets. making stock market more used to and common
investment platform for people so they can get motivated to invest in real stocks.
