About Us

BullPOV is a skill-based platform where users predict whether a stock will rise or fall on the same trading day.

Our Vision

Shaping a Smarter and More Exciting Investing Culture

At BullPOV, our mission is to transform how people, especially the digital-savvy generation, connect with the stock market. We aim to make the world of stocks more thrilling, relatable, and accessible. Rather than acting as a traditional brokerage, BullPOV introduces a unique, skill-based platform where users engage by predicting the directional movements of various stocks. It’s not just a game. It’s a powerful learning experience.

By encouraging users to make thoughtful and informed predictions, we help them stay updated with financial news, understand economic trends, and observe real-time market behavior. Our platform serves as a bridge between curiosity and confidence, turning passive observers into active participants in India’s financial ecosystem. Over time, we aspire to inspire users to transition into actual investing, equipped with stronger awareness, greater enthusiasm, and a deeper understanding of how markets work. BullPOV is here to ignite a movement where financial literacy meets excitement.

We’re the pioneers of stock-based skill gaming.

How it works?

Here’s a detailed look at how you can effectively engage with and make the most out of your experience on BullPOV.

Spot

Pick Up Stocks

Start by discovering stocks that are trending or catching attention. Use your instinct or curiosity to choose the ones that interest you. This is your first step toward understanding market momentum.

Study

Read charts, news, patterns

Take a moment to look into basic news, sentiment, or trends about the stock. A little research goes a long way in sharpening your prediction. It’s where your analytical skills start building up.

Guess

Make your directional call

Predict whether the stock will go up or down by the end of the day. This isn’t luck—it’s a reflection of your awareness and judgement. The more informed you are, the better your prediction gets.

Play

Submit your prediction

Place your prediction with a simple tap and compete with others. It’s quick, engaging, and challenges your market intuition. Every round makes you smarter and more market-ready.

Wait & Watch

See how it performs

Keep an eye on your prediction as the market moves throughout the day. The real test ends when the stock market closes at 3:30 PM. Whether you win or lose, each result sharpens your instinct and market sense.
