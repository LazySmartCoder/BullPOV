    Frequently Asked Questions

    Below are some frequently asked questions (FAQs) that provide detailed insights and clarity about our platform, its features, and how you can make the most of it.

    What is Bullpov and how does it work?

    BullPOV is India’s first skill-based stock opinion platform where users predict whether a stock will go up or down by the end of the day. It’s not trading or investing, it’s a fun, competitive environment built around real-time market trends. You apply your insights, compete with others, and improve your financial instincts, all without owning a single stock.

    Is Bullpov a stock trading or brokerage app?

    No, Bullpov is not a stock trading or brokerage platform. You don’t buy or sell real stocks here. You simply use your skills to predict stock movements and compete for rewards, helping you learn market behavior in a fun.

    Do I need prior stock market knowledge to play?

    Not necessarily! While some market awareness helps, Bullpov is beginner-friendly. Over time, you’ll naturally learn how to read trends, follow financial news, and sharpen your analytical skills, all while playing.

    Is Bullpov legal and safe to use in India?

    Yes, Bullpov is completely legal and operates under the category of skill-based gaming. We don’t offer real trading or gambling, instead, it's a platform built around financial literacy and prediction-based competition.

    How does Bullpov help me in real-life stock investing?

    Bullpov sharpens your financial instincts by encouraging you to analyze market trends and predict stock directions. Over time, this builds confidence, improves decision-making, and prepares you to engage with the real stock market more skillfully, all in a fun, risk-free environment.
