BullPOV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2025

Welcome to BullPOV EdTech Company (“we,” “our,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use our platform at BullPOV, and all services associated with it.

By registering or using our platform, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who Can Use Our Platform

  • Only Indian citizens aged 14 years or older can use BullPOV.
  • Users must accept these terms at the time of registration.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Personal details: Name, phone number, email address, date of birth
  • Device & browser info: IP address, browser type, OS
  • Payment info: Mode of payment, transaction ID, wallet history
  • Usage data: Trade history, login frequency, predictions, etc.

3. Why We Collect This Information

  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To enable simulated trading and skill-based games
  • To provide educational market simulations
  • To process wallet deposits and payouts
  • To send updates, marketing messages, and results
  • To ensure security, fraud prevention, and compliance

4. Wallet & Payment

  • You can deposit money via any accepted payment mode.
  • Payouts are processed only through our internal wallet, which may take up to 4 business days to reflect.
  • Users can withdraw anytime via the wallet dashboard.

5. Trading Rules

  • Trades can be placed from 4:30 PM to 9:00 AM the next day.
  • Once a trade is placed, no cancellation or refund is allowed.
  • Delisted stocks will be removed from the platform.
  • We do not take commissions from winnings.
  • 490+ BSE stocks and Nifty indices (NSE) are listed.

6. Reward Distribution Mechanism

BullPOV calculates winnings based on:

  • The percentage share of a user's prediction in their prediction pool.
  • That percentage is used to calculate the reward portion from the opposing pool, which is then redistributed among the winners.

7. Security & Misuse

  • Fake accounts, fraud, or misuse can result in account suspension or legal action.
  • We reserve the right to ban users found violating our terms.
  • All legal matters fall under the jurisdiction of the Karnataka High Court.

8. No Liability

  • BullPOV is not responsible for any market fluctuations or inaccurate predictions.
  • We do not offer any compensation in case of loss, property damage, or loss of life.
  • This platform is a game of skill and market education simulation, not actual financial trading.

9. Marketing Communication

  • By registering, you consent to receive emails, SMS, or other marketing and transactional communications.
  • You can opt out anytime using the link provided in such emails.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • View or update your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Withdraw consent for marketing emails

Contact us at support@bullpov.com to exercise these rights.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. You will be notified of significant changes via email or in-app notifications.

Cookie Policy (Part of Privacy Policy)

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device to help us improve your experience.

Why We Use Cookies

  • Remember your login preferences
  • Track activity on our platform (analytics)
  • Improve performance and usability

Cookie Types

  • Essential Cookies: Required for login, trading, and session management
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand user behavior on our platform
  • Functional Cookies: Store preferences like dark mode, etc.

How to Control Cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. However, some features of our platform may stop working properly.
